What's included

  • measure_results — every cached per-image metric value.
  • expert_annotations — original ratings shipped with the dataset (e.g. MOS), if any.
  • pairwise_annotations — contributed 2AFC comparisons and rankings.
  • bradley_terry — continuous quality scores derived from the comparisons (β ± SE).

Annotator identities are pseudonymised (annotator_001, …) — no names or emails are included, so the export is safe to publish.
